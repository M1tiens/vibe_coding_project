## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** There was a problem with understanding the context and products purpose.
- **What confused them:** The context and the amount of data. It was too overwhelming for the user at first.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** A dashboard with data that was not very understandable for the user at first glance. Need better context definition.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** I think there was a big gap on this first iteration for what was produced from what was intended. The data was good but the context needs to be better explained and the UX/UI experience needs to be improved. It's too much all over the place.
